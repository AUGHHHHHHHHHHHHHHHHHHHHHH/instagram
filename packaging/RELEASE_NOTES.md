## Install on your Mac

1. Download **Reelvoice-…-macOS-arm64.dmg** below (Apple Silicon Macs: M1 and newer).
2. Open it and drag **Reelvoice** into **Applications**.
3. First launch only: macOS will say it can't verify the app, because it isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate.
   Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Reelvoice. Then open it again.

   If you prefer the Terminal instead:  `xattr -cr /Applications/Reelvoice.app`

Everything is inside the app, including ffmpeg. Your voiceovers and reels are saved to **Movies → Reelvoice**.

Intel Macs: the app is built for Apple Silicon only. On an Intel Mac use the command-line version (see the README).
